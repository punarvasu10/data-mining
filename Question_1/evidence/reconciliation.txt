DIAGNOSIS
=========
March:
All 12 stores have 31 days of source data.
Pipeline equals raw source revenue.
Therefore this is a source/finance reconciliation difference.
Exact underlying cause is not established by the available monthly finance data.

July:
S07 has no source exports for 2024-07-09, 2024-07-10 and 2024-07-11.
Pipeline equals the available raw source data.
Vendor notes state that S07 had a three-day export gap and finance used
phoned-in numbers.
Therefore this is a source-data gap, not a pipeline calculation bug.

December:
All 12 stores have 31 days of source data.
Pipeline equals raw source revenue.
The remaining difference is only INR 50.48.
Therefore this is a small source/finance reconciliation difference;
no pipeline bug was identified.

Pipeline bug fixed during development:
1. Bill-level DISCOUNT rows were being dropped by an INNER JOIN to products.
2. Product resolution was changed to LEFT JOIN.
3. Revenue was restricted to SALE, RETURN, DISCOUNT and VOID.
4. TAX and TENDER are excluded from revenue.
5. Final fact row count: 1,120,924.
